Dear Vahiba...

You are not just my sister.

You are one of those beautiful parts of my life that I can never replace.

Through all the little arguments, the silly moments, the laughter, the memories and everything in between...

I will always be grateful that Allah blessed me with a sister like you.

No matter how much life changes, I hope you always know that you have a special place in my heart.

And whenever you look back at these memories, I hope they remind you of how deeply you are loved. 💗

A sister is a little bit of childhood that can never be lost. 💕 AND YOU WILL ALWAYS BE MY LITTLE PIECE OF HOME.
❤️

With all my love, always.

FOR MY DEAR SISTER
